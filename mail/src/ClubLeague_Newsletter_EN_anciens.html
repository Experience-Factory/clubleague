It's been a while since we last saw you on track, and we'd love to change that. Tuesday nights in Eupen now have a new rendezvous, the Club League, and your Gold licence gives you a place in it.

You don't need to be one of the fastest in the paddock: in the Club League, you race against drivers at your own level.

How it works

Every Tuesday counts

Every Tuesday you race 3 sprints. Each sprint earns you points, and your best results of the quarter make up your standings. The quarter ends with the Grande Finale on Tuesday 29 December.

Two categories

Your level, your standings

 Performance
Drivers with at least 3 podiums in the Tuesday sprints in 2025 and 2026.
 Challenger
Everyone else, new drivers included, with their own standings, their own places in the final and their own rewards.

Everyone races together, but each category has its own standings. And if you keep collecting podiums, you move up to Performance without waiting for the end of the quarter.

What's at stake

Your place in the Grande Finale

The top 14 Performance and top 10 Challenger drivers earn their place in the Grande Finale on 29 December. In each category, the podium of the quarter takes home a reward worth racing for:

 Performance
Your entry to top karting races: KWC, BNC...
 Challenger
An unforgettable experience: a Formula 1 Grand Prix, the 24 Hours of Spa...

Plus the title of Autumn 2026 Club League Champion.

It's not too late

11 Tuesdays to go

The autumn season started on 6 October. To make the final, 4 Tuesdays are enough in Challenger (your 12 best sprints count) and 5 in Performance (15 best sprints).
